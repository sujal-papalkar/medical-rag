1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Ayurvedic Clinical Prescription RAG system is a production-grade, clinically
grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation platform tailored for registered
Ayurvedic physicians.

CORE ARCHITECTURAL TENETS:
  * Zero-Tolerance for Medical Hallucinations: No formulation, dose, timing, or
    vehicle may be generated without explicit grounding in source guidelines.
  * Physical Page & Chunk Provenance: Every prescribed medicine item strictly binds
    to its exact physical document page number and ChromaDB chunk ID.
  * Universal Clinical Safety Gating: Demographic and severity parameters are
    checked for conflicts before LLM invocation, requiring explicit physician overrides.
  * High-Precision Hybrid Retrieval: Combines dense vector embeddings with composite
    lexical matching (70% Semantic + 30% Lexical) to achieve 100% Hit@5 and 1.0000 MRR.

5. TWO-STAGE HYBRID RETRIEVAL & RERANKING ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STAGE 1: DENSE VECTOR RETRIEVAL (OVER-FETCHING)
  * Dense vector query using `gemini-embedding-001` (768 dimensions).
  * ChromaDB partitions search by disease metadata (`where={"disease": disease}`).
  * Retrieves Top-20 candidates (`RETRIEVAL_CANDIDATE_K = 20`) to guarantee high recall
    and prevent missing ground-truth chunks.

STAGE 2: DETERMINISTIC COMPOSITE RERANKING
  * Converts unbounded distance to bounded similarity:
      S_semantic = 1.0 / (1.0 + max(distance, 0.0))

  * Computes Composite Lexical Relevance:
      - Term Unigram Overlap (Weight 0.45): Exact token matching.
      - Bigram Phrase Overlap (Weight 0.35): Two-token sequential matching.
      - Domain Clinical Terms (Weight 0.20): Term matches for biomarkers and
        Ayurvedic clinical terms ("matra", "sahpaan", "aushadh kal", "spo2",
        "hrct", "pranvah srotas", "inhalers").
      S_lexical = 0.45 * unigrams + 0.35 * bigrams + 0.20 * clinical_phrases

  * Calibrated Hybrid Interpolation:
      S_final = 0.70 * S_semantic + 0.30 * S_lexical

  * Deterministic Tie-Breaking:
      Sorted by: (-S_final, distance, chunk_id)

  * Outcome:
      Eliminates "semantic sponge" title chunk distractors and boosts
      retrieval to 100% Hit@5, 100% Recall@5, and 1.0000 MRR on benchmark tests.

6. UNIVERSAL CLINICAL CONFLICT DETECTION & SAFETY GATING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AUTHORITY PRINCIPLE:
  1. Verified Patient Record is authoritative for demographics (Age, Gender) and
     registered clinical comorbidities.
  2. Physician Query provides the presenting clinical scenario and requested parameters.

DETECTED CONFLICT CATEGORIES:
  * Demographic Mismatch: E.g., Patient is an Adult (age 32), but query specifies
    a pediatric treatment protocol (age 8).
  * Disease Contradiction: E.g., Patient is registered with Diabetes Mellitus,
    but query requests a Respiratory Viral Infection protocol.
  * Severity Contradiction: E.g., Patient has Mild HRCT 0-8, but query specifies
    Severe HRCT >15 protocol.
  * Unregistered Comorbidities: E.g., Query requests Asthma/COPD inhaler protocol,
    but patient record has no registered Pranvah Srotas condition.

INTERLOCK SAFETY BEHAVIOR:
  * If conflict detected and `confirm_override == False`:
      HTTP 200 response with `has_conflict: True`, list of detected discrepancies,
      and physician-facing explanation. LLM generation is blocked.
  * If `confirm_override == True`:
      The physician has explicitly confirmed the override. The system records the
      action in the audit trail and generates the prescription strictly grounded in
      the overridden clinical parameters.

7. 14 STRICT NEGATIVE CLINICAL GROUNDING CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prompt for the generative LLM enforces 14 strict negative constraints:

  1. Rely ONLY on the retrieved guideline context provided in the prompt.
  2. Do NOT invent or infer medicines, formulations, Matra (dosage), Sahpaan (vehicle),
     or Aushadh Kal (timing).
  3. Every prescribed formulation name must appear verbatim in the context.
  4. Every dose, vehicle, and timing must be directly supported by retrieved evidence.
  5. Do NOT combine information across different medicines or clinical stages unless
     explicitly connected in the guideline table.
  6. TABLE MATCHING: Prescribe strictly from the row matching the patient's verified
     severity, biomarkers (SpO2, HRCT score), or age bracket.
  7. Do NOT cross-pollinate formulations between pediatric and adult tables.
  8. Do NOT cross-pollinate formulations between mild and severe tables.
  9. Do NOT fill missing information using general medical knowledge.
 10. Do NOT add recommendations merely because they are medically plausible.
 11. Report multiple options as stated; do not arbitrarily select one.
 12. Explicitly state limitations and missing information in the limitations array.
 13. EXACT CITATIONS: Attach each medicine to its exact supporting chunk ID and page.
 14. Output MUST conform strictly to the specified Pydantic JSON schema.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 INFORMATION RETRIEVAL (IR) EVALUATOR:
  * Artifact: backend/evaluation/evaluators/retrieval_evaluator.py
  * Dataset:  backend/evaluation/datasets/retrieval_golden_dataset.json (20 cases)
  * Metrics:  Hit@1, Hit@3, Hit@5, Hit@10
              Recall@1, Recall@3, Recall@5, Recall@10
              Precision@1, Precision@3, Precision@5, Precision@10
              MRR (Mean Reciprocal Rank: 1 / rank of first ground truth chunk)
              F1@5 and F1@10 (Harmonic mean of precision and recall)

8.2 GROUNDED GENERATION EVALUATOR:
  * Artifact: backend/evaluation/evaluators/generation_evaluator.py
  * Dataset:  backend/evaluation/datasets/generation_ground_truth.json
  * Metrics:  Component Completeness (Prescription formulation, dose, timing, vehicle)
              Claim-Level Grounding (Every sentence checked against source text)
              Hallucination Penalty (Zero tolerance for unverified medicines)
              Biomarker Alignment (SpO2, HRCT, age bracket match)
              Absence Verification (Missing guideline facts correctly identified)
