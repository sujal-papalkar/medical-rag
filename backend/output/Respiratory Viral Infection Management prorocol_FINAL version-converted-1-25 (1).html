Ayurved Rasayani's

Guidelines for the Management of Respiratory Viral Infections

Dear Doctors,

The human race is going through challenging times since last couple of years. A new respiratory virus has emerged and is causing immense pressure on the healthcare infrastructure. Each stake holder is contributing towards finding solutions to overcome the pandemic.

As we all know viruses have always been a challenge for scientists. It was really inspiring to see how the scientific community has redirected their research efforts to tackle the COVID 19 pandemic. These multidisciplinary efforts helped us immensely to understand the epidemiology, biological features, dynamics and clinical presentation of the new corona virus.

Respiratory viral infections are not new for the mankind. Epidemiological data suggests that there are several deaths every year due to Influenza. We all know that Ayurveda has played a vital role in treating respiratory viral infections. But it was very important to understand this virus before reaching to any conclusion.

Now let us see what Ayurveda Rasayani has done to find solution to this global health challenge. Ayurveda Rasayani has always believed in Research based approach while developing any therapeutics for chronic and critical illness. As soon as we learnt about the pandemic in China, we started our research activities to ensure that we will be ready with some satisfactory solution for COVID when it takes form of a pandemic in India.

Our scientists started studying the scientific literature coming from China, in few weeks there were more quality literature from the west which gave us further understanding about the disease. At the same time there was strong communication between our scientists and Ayurveda experts. When scientists were busy studying scientific literature, the vaidyas were going through Ayurveda classics. There were several interdisciplinary meetings within our team and this exercise gave us a good understanding about the disease. Along with this literary research we also conducted in vitro evaluation of Ayurveda compounds for studying antiviral activity against the Novel corona virus. The results were very encouraging. This was followed by clinical evaluation of our formulations. We conducted randomized double blind, placebo controlled clinical trial to evaluate efficacy and safety of Ayurveda therapy. This study was conducted in dedicated COVID specialty hospital. In this study Ayurveda medicine showed statistically significant improvement in terms of reduction of inflammatory markers as compared to standard and care. Clinical trial was conducted in 200 patients. Parallel to clinical study our vaidyas at Rasayu Ayurveda clinic were successfully treating patients of COVID with Ayurveda. Some of our patients were treated exclusively with Ayurveda whereas; some received Ayurveda along with Conventional care.

Our vaidyas treated more than 2000 COVID patients ranging from those with mild symptoms to those on ventilators. I was fortunate to be a part of central COVID 19 task force. Also, I was a member of advisory meeting with Prime minister of India. These platforms gave me a great opportunity to interact with various experts and understand their point of views as well. All the knowledge gained through the above process gave us immense understanding about this disease.

Members of our team along with me were in constant touch with some of our professional colleagues who were using our medicines for treating COVID. These vaidyas constantly shared their success stories with us which again boosted our confidence in our approach of treating COVID. Many vaidyas used medicines of Ayurveda Rasayani during the pandemic. Ayurveda Rasayani distributed 5 lacs Capsules of its proprietary medicine Pranvir in the year 2021. These highly specialized Rasayanas are formulated as per assured quality parameters of highest precision. E.g., Bhasmas used in Rasayanas are shataputi

Recently we could see new strains of COVID-19 emerging and there is a threat that further new strains will keep emerging. Vaccines are not seen to be offering satisfactory protection against newer strains. In such situation the approach of Ayurveda seems to be logical, practical and eternal. Ayurveda's approach is not a virus specific but related to the involved strotas, affected dhatus and vitiated doshas. This approach can be effective against any respiratory viral infections of past, present and probably future as well. With an aim to provide guidance to vaidyas in management of respiratory viral infections, Ayurveda Rasayani has prepared this document. This document is an essence of our vast experience of treating thousands of patients with respiratory viral infections of different strains. Besides my active involvement in preparing this document there was a valuable contributions from our team members like Dr.Vineeta Bendale, Dr.Ketaki Jagtap, Dr.Rutuja Pandav, Dr.Subodh Jagdale, Dr.Aadish Kale and Dr.Avinash Kadam.

I am thankful to scientific stalwards Dr.Ulhas wagh, Dr. Dinesh Amalnerkar, Dr.Kalpana Joshi, Dr.Achyut Joshi and Dr.Padma Shastry who continuously enlightened us with their wisdom which helped us to understand this disease better.

I am confident that this document will be of great help for all the vaidyas in treating various respiratory viral infections. Ayurveda Rasayani is committed to provide vaidyas with most authentic and efficacious medicines which along with the knowledge in this document will be of great help to vaidyas in the war against respiratory virus. This being concise form of ready reckoner and considering pressure of time, we were not in position to mention ayurvedic view of each condition.

Thanking You, Dr.Yogesh Bendale CMD, Ayurveda Rasayani

DISCLAIMER

  • The document is based on experience of managing various Respiratory Viral Infection of different origin using Ayurveda medicines. However, this document is no way claim to treat any particular viral infection.
  • These guidelines should only serve as a supportive document for Ayurveda physicians to manage any kind of Respiratory Viral Infection and its complications.
  • These guidelines are exclusively meant for use only by registered Ayurveda physicians. It is strictly not intended for self-medication by patients.
  • Physicians are advised to use these guidelines only after a detail consultation, history taking and examination of patients.
  • This document in no way means to discourage the use of any other system of medicine. The physician using these guidelines has to use his own knowledge and experience in deciding to offer best possible treatment for the patient.
  • Physicians have their own discretion to select drugs based upon the stage of disease, complexity of symptoms, availability of medicines in their locality etc. and dose and duration has to be decided according to condition of the patient. List provided gives an idea about the different group of choice of medication available for the said conditions. Physicians may select medicines from the list provided in this document or other than the list as per the complaints and availability.
  • Patients who are already taking some medications for other illnesses should be advised to continue those medications after thorough examination.
  • Use of medicines mentioned in this document is subjected to Epidemic Disease and Disaster Management Act if applicable. It is the treating physician's responsibility to check with the local applicable and prevalent laws. Contributors will not be held responsible in any way for the same
