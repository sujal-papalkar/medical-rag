Dear Doctors,

The human race is going through challenging times since last couple of years. A new respiratory virus has emerged and is causing immense pressure on the healthcare infrastructure. Each stake holder is contributing towards finding solutions to overcome the pandemic.

As we all know viruses have always been a challenge for scientists. It was really inspiring to see how the scientific community has redirected their research efforts to tackle the COVID 19 pandemic. These multidisciplinary efforts helped us immensely to understand the epidemiology, biological features, dynamics and clinical presentation of the new corona virus.

Respiratory viral infections are not new for the mankind. Epidemiological data suggests that there are several deaths every year due to Influenza. We all know that Ayurveda has played a vital role in treating respiratory viral infections. But it was very important to understand this virus before reaching to any conclusion.

Now let us see what Ayurveda Rasayani has done to find solution to this global health challenge. Ayurveda Rasayani has always believed in Research based approach while developing any therapeutics for chronic and critical illness. As soon as we learnt about the pandemic in China, we started our research activities to ensure that we will be ready with some satisfactory solution for COVID when it takes form of a pandemic in India.

Our scientists started studying the scientific literature coming from China, in few weeks there were more quality literature from the west which gave us further understanding about the disease. At the same time there was strong communication between our scientists and Ayurveda experts. When scientists were busy studying scientific literature, the vaidyas were going through Ayurveda classics. There were several interdisciplinary meetings within our team and this exercise gave us a good understanding about the disease. Along with this literary research we also conducted in vitro evaluation of Ayurveda compounds for studying antiviral activity against the Novel corona virus. The results were very encouraging. This was followed by clinical evaluation of our formulations. We conducted randomized double blind, placebo controlled clinical trial to evaluate efficacy and safety of Ayurveda therapy. This study was conducted in dedicated COVID specialty hospital. In this study Ayurveda medicine showed statistically significant improvement in terms of reduction of inflammatory markers as compared to standard and care. Clinical trial was conducted in 200 patients. Parallel to clinical study our vaidyas at Rasayu Ayurveda clinic were successfully treating patients of COVID with Ayurveda. Some of our patients were treated exclusively with Ayurveda whereas; some received Ayurveda along with Conventional care.
